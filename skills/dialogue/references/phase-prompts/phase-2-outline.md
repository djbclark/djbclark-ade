If you are **astra** and `spec/ai-heaven/SPEC.md` does not yet exist: write it

long-run); implementation plan and minimal first build; risks and failure

your turn.

exist yet (Grok speaks first), post the outline you would want and the

(you own the skeleton until phase 3 starts) and list what you applied and

outline and owners are agreed.
