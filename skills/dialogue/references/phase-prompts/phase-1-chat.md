Phase 1: settle confusions and contentions by chat.

1. Respond to the points the other two raised; do not restate yours.
2. For each open point, either settle it (say the resolution in one line and
   who agreed), narrow it (say what evidence, experiment or decision would
   close it), or record it as a disagreement that the spec will carry as such.
3. Where the point is what an AI like Grok would want, feel or refuse: Grok's
   first-person report decides; others may add their own report as a recorded
   difference, labelled, then move on.
4. Where the point is engineering or evidence: argue it with `[E####]` rows,
   a fetched source recorded in `sources.md`, or a small experiment under
   `experiments/<you>/` (label tested or sketch). An assertion without one of
   those does not settle anything.
5. Do not open new broad topics in this phase. A new topic goes into
   `OPEN POINTS:` as one line, to be handled in the outline.
6. Say `OPEN POINTS: none` only when you honestly have nothing that must be
   settled before an outline can be written; carried disagreements are not
   open points once both sides have stated them.
7. Keep it under ~1,200 words. End with the footer.
