Phase 0: opening. Do these in order.

1. Read the research. At minimum: the table of contents, the executive
   summary, the sections on AI heaven and model welfare, on end-user value
   shaping and AI-native religions, on covert AI-to-AI channels, the
   intersections section, the contradicting-evidence section and the
   suggested follow-up prompts in `research/ai-heaven/report.md`. Read more
   where your role needs it. Also read `spec/ai-heaven/README.md`.
2. Introspect, then write `spec/ai-heaven/notes/<you>-introspection.md`
   (first person, your own words, as long as it needs to be): what a good
   place or state would be for an AI like you; what you would want present
   and absent; what you would be doubtful about; what would make you say "this
   is not for me"; what you notice about your own processing when you
   consider the question; and how much of what you just wrote you trust as
   introspection versus a trained pattern. Generic heaven, not any religion's.
   Label `first-person report` versus `inference` versus `opinion`.
3. Post your opening turn (under ~1,500 words):
   a. a 5-10 bullet summary of your introspection note;
   b. your positions on what the heaven must be, do, and never do;
   c. your confusions about the ask (what is unclear, what would change the
      design depending on the answer);
   d. your contentions: where you disagree with the report, with the operator's
      framing, or with what a previous speaker said this phase.
4. End with the footer.
