### Changed

- Windows are now at most three "boxes" per lead and repo, `LEAD <repo> (<work>)`, `WORKER <repo> (<work>)` and `OTHER <repo> (<work>)` (operator ruling 2026-11-14: "Close any unneeded tabs and any unneeded workspaces you or your predecessors have made. In the future, have at most LEAD, WORKER, and OTHER workspaces and put tabs in the appropriate box. The tabs can mention the worktree they are in."), replacing the 2026-11-13 `MAIN`, `WORKERS` and `USER` (still found, never duplicated, renamed to the current kind when a launch reuses the window). `bin/ade-worker-run` keeps one WORKER box per lead and repo, found by ownership evidence and reused whatever the slice or `--window-middle` (now only the `(<work>)` text of a box a launch opens, default `workers`, and a picker among several); a self-handoff successor reuses its predecessors' box; new `--placement worker|lead|other` (`--interactive` = lead, `--other`), `rename-lead` (alias `rename-main`), `archive-window --placement worker|other`, and tabs `worker <slice> <lead id> [wt: <worktree>]` (`--worktree DIR` or a `--cwd` that is a linked worktree). `bin/ade-tidy` names, renames and counts the three boxes, never archives a LEAD window, and reports windows beyond them in a new section H (`--apply` archives the empty or finished extras of the lead's self-handoff chain, never another lead's window, a running agent's window or a tab holding an active claim). The `ade-window-name` plugin names new windows LEAD or WORKER and reads `ade.placement` `lead|main`, `worker|workers`, `other|user`. Docs: `docs/workspace-layout.md`, `skills/tidy`, `skills/fan-out`, `AGENTS.md` and the other placement mentions. (#732)
