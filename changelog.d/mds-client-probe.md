### Added

- `load-watch` mds probe (2026-11-01, approved by the lead): when root's `mds` has used 0.5+ cores over consecutive samples spanning 2 minutes (`LW_MDS_CORES`, `LW_MDS_SUSTAIN_S`; a gap over `LW_MDS_MAX_GAP_S`, 90 s, restarts the span), one line per 30 minutes (`LW_MDS_COOLDOWN_S`) goes to the load-watch log with mds, Finder and the six busiest other processes. Processes are matched and logged by program name (first token's basename, `[A-Za-z0-9._+-]` only), never by arguments, so a path argument cannot pose as mds or Finder and no argument value (a token, a password flag) reaches the log. It needs no root, pings nobody and signals nothing; `load-watch status` shows its cooldown as 30 minutes. Finder's share is the cheap tell (the cause on 2026-11-01 was Finder re-listing `/private/tmp`, found by hand with root-only `sample` and `fs_usage`); when the root helper `/usr/local/libexec/djbclark/mds-xpc-clients` (source `macos/mds-xpc-clients` in site-public-djbclark; one NOPASSWD sudoers line, no arguments) is installed, the line also lists up to six XPC clients of mds as `ports pid name` through `sudo -n` (20 s timeout; any failure leaves the line as it was). Test: `tests/test_load_watch_mds_probe.py` (#231).
