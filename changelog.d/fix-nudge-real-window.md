### Changed

- The context-size nudge (`claude/hooks/context_size_nudge.py`) computes each session's real compaction wall at run time (`compaction_wall`: the lower of `CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_COMPACT_WINDOW` / `modelSettings` / `autoCompactWindow` and the model's own window, less the 33k buffer; unreadable or unknown settings keep today's 267k) and derives every handoff tier from it: the "hand off at the next task completion" note fires at wall - 80k (187k on a 267k wall, was a fixed 120k, which made a lead hand itself off at ~150k with 117k left), the hard tier at wall - 50k by total only, Hermes paging at max(400k, hard tier). The `ASK_GROWTH` trigger, which fired the hard tier at ~175k, is removed. The messages print the computed wall and the tokens left, keep the 2026-11-14 "hand off yourself" ruling, and say a finished task alone is no reason to hand off or compact below the boundary tier. `bin/context-watch` now calls the hook's `compaction_wall` and `_tiers` (one copy), so its ceiling falls back to 267k, not a 200k guess, when nothing is known.
